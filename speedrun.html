Segment 1: Vengeful Spirit

Geo Required: 50

  • 1. Collect Fury of the Fallen (pogo the spikes) to unlock Salubra's first charm notch.
  • 2. Gather 50 geo on the way to the Forgotten Crossroads Stag Station by destroying geo rocks.
  • 3. Unlock the Stag Station, bench to heal if needed.
  • 4. Defeat False Knight, collect the City Crest and his geo chest.
  • 5. Get Vengeful Spirit, pick up Soul Catcher and equip it at the bench, then head to Greenpath.

Segment 2: Mothwing Cloak

Geo Required: 50

  • 1. Head to the Greenpath Stag Station, defeating the Vengefly King and the double Moss Knights for their geo on the way, as well as picking up the Wanderer's Journal behind the breakable wall.
  • 2. Bench at the Stag Station but do not unlock the stag.
  • 3. Defeat Hornet, then save and quit immediately after picking up Mothwing Cloak

Segment 3: Mantis Claw

Geo Required: none

  • 1. Head to Mantis Claw, stopping for the Stag Station bench and Hallownest Seal on the way.
  • 2. Head to the City of Tears, collecting the Journal and double geo rocks on the way.

Segment 4: Soul Sanctum

Geo Required: 600

  • 1. Bench and grab the Nail Upgrade, then save and quit back to the bench.
  • 2. Head to Soul Sanctum, grabbing the Hallownest Seal on the way. If you are missing geo at any point, sell a relic to Lemm.
  • 3. Purchase and sit on the bench, then go through Soul Sanctum, grabbing Spell Twister, defeating Soul Master, grabbing the geo chest and the Hallownest Seal afterwards.
  • 4. Sit at the bench again. Equip Spell Twister. Go to Lemm and sell everything. Save and quit back to the bench. Try not to die with this geo.
  • 5. Collect the Simple Key and then stag to Forgotten Crossroads.

Segment 5: Gruz Mother

Geo Required: 2260

  • 1. Sit at the bench. Defeat Gruz Mother, speak to Sly, and purchase Steady Body, Shaman Stone, and one charm notch from Salubra. Save and quit back to the bench.
  • 2. Equip Spell Twister and Shaman Stone. Go to Dirtmouth and purchase the Lumafly Lantern from Sly. Save and quit back to the bench again.
  • 3. Head to Crystal Peaks.

Segment 6: Crystal Peaks

Geo Required: none

  • 1. Go straight to Crystal Heart. There is an optional safety bench which you can fork off to right before going up in Crystal Peaks, just head right instead first.
  • 2. Go straight to Descending Dark, and then continue to Dream Nail.
  • 3. After waking up in the Resting Grounds, go to the Stag Station and head back to the City Storerooms.

Segment 7: Isma's Tear and Lurien

Geo Required: 300

  • 1. Head towards the Royal Waterways, using the Simple Key to enter, and benching inside.
  • 2. Defeat Dung Defender, hit the lever, and grab Isma's Tear.
  • 3. At (9), perform a background object pogo skip below the Watcher Knights to access them. Simply jump, wait until you are at the peak of your jump height, then pogo the object and walljump off of the wall. If you miss twice, then you can exit and re-enter the room from the left elevator to respawn the objects.
  • 4. NOTE: If you cannot do this skip, simply head to Broken Vessel, bench at the purchasable bench on your way there, collect Monarch Wings, and save and quit back to the bench. Continue through the route normally, but use the secret entrance to Beast's Den to save some time later.
  • 5. Head to Watcher Knights, sitting at the Spire bench along the way. Hit down the Chandelier, accessible by breaking the ceiling to the left of the boss room before fighting them.
  • 6. Go and kill Lurien. On your way down, collect the geo chest.
  • 7. Take the Kings Station Stag to Dirtmouth.

Segment 8: Monomon

Geo Required: none

  • 1. Head to Teacher's Archives. In the Teacher's Archives, grab the safety bench at the top and then fight Uumuu and kill Monomon.
  • 2. Exit the Teacher's Archives and sit on the safety bench again.
  • 3. Head left towards Queen's Gardens.

Segment 9: Herrah

Geo Required: 400

  • 1. Use the acid entrance to enter Queen's Gardens.
  • 2. Purchase the bench before Deepnest and sit on it, then head to Beast's Den and kill Herrah.
  • 3. Take the Distant Village Stag Station to Dirtmouth.

Segment 10: The Hollow Knight

Geo Required: none

Go to the Hollow Knight. Do not stop for the bench unless you need to heal as you will automatically spawn there after dying regardless.
